## Legal screen (outside my brief, but it changes the operating design)
- Ley 643 de 2001, art. 27 defines *rifas* as drawing "premios en especie" among holders of numbered boletas sold at a fixed price by an authorized operator [S, read via summary] (#14).
- Decreto 1486 de 2024 (13 Dec 2024) [P] (#13):
  - "Se prohíbe la rifa de bienes usados y las rifas con premios en dinero."
  - Gestores (natural or legal persons) may not run a raffle "que no sea operada por un tercero" (an authorized *operador*).
  - A gestor may hold at most two raffle dates per month.
  - *Derechos de explotación* are 14% of gross revenue, computed on 100% of the emitted ticket value.
  - Results may come from a traditional lottery, or from a random-number generator limited to six digits.
  - Sales may be physical or via web and social media, with a 12-month transition for manual sales.
- Derived: 14% of 1,000 × COP 6,000 is COP 840,000.
- Coljuegos asked Meta to block 289 Facebook/Instagram profiles for raffles (Dec 2024) and cites sanctions up to 100 SMMLV plus Código Penal art. 312 [P] (#16). An influencer fine of COP 116M was reported in Aug 2026 [S] (#18).
- Operator requirements are heavy, for example COP 6.35 billion minimum working capital [S] (#17).
- Not verified: whether the transition has ended, whether any operator is authorized, or any exemption for small personal raffles [U]. Reading the decree's text, a cash-prize raffle of a used bike does not fit [U].

## B. Raffle mechanics and best practices

### B.1 Formats and non-power-of-ten sizes
The premio mayor number is 0000–9999. A 3-digit raffle reads the last three digits, so Boyacá's 9882 gives 882 [P] (#3). The arithmetic below is mine [U]:

| Tickets | Rule | Odds per number |
|---|---|---|
| 100 | last 2 digits | 1% |
| 1,000 | last 3 digits | 0.1% |
| 10,000 | all 4 digits | 0.01% |
| 500 / 250 / 200 / 125 | last 3 digits mod N (for 500, subtract 500 if ≥500) | uniform (2, 4, 5, 8 outcomes per 1,000) |
| **300** | last 3 digits mod 300 | **not uniform**: numbers 000–099 have 4/1000, the other 200 have 3/1000 (33% higher) |
| 300 | 4-digit result mod 300; results 9900–9999 trigger a redraw | uniform (33/9,900); 1% redraw |

Recommendation: restructure the mini-raffle as 250 numbers (about COP 2,400 each) or 200 numbers (COP 3,000 each) to keep COP 600,000 gross. Alternatively drop it, since its best-case net is COP 400,000 (600,000 − 200,000 prize).

### B.2 Winning number not sold
The rule must be fixed before the first sale. Changing it after the draw is "indistinguishable from a trick" [S] (#20), and the criterion "debe estar previsto antes del sorteo" [S] (#19). Mexican guides call the next scheduled draw the most common convention [S] (#20). I found no Colombian source [U].

| Policy | Pros | Cons | Buyer reaction [U] |
|---|---|---|---|
| Organizer keeps prize | simple | incentive to leave numbers unsold | negative |
| Roll to next ordinary draw (once) | prize stays alive, widely cited | delay of 3–7 days | generally accepted |
| Closest sold number | quick | unequal odds near gaps | contested |
| Wait until sold out | fairest | open-ended; kills momentum [S] (#21); "permanent" raffles are banned [S] (#14) | impatience |
| Minimum sales or refund | clear | refund workload | accepted if stated |

If 90% of numbers sell, there is a 10% chance the winner is unsold [derived].

### B.3 Apartados, fiado, unpaid on draw day
- Counting reservations as sales, paying without identification, parallel seller lists and drawing with pending reservations are the classic errors [S] (#19).
- Recommendation: reservations expire in 24–48 h.
- Recommendation: grid states are Libre, Apartado, Pagado and Anulado.
- Recommendation: no fiado.
- Recommendation: an unpaid number on draw day is void and handled as unsold. Never accept payment after the result.

### B.4 Secondary prizes
- **Número invertido.** Roughly 100 of the 1,000 three-digit numbers are palindromes where it adds no chance [derived].
- **Weekly early-bird draws.** They need a separate, pre-announced, verifiable source and only paid tickets.
- **Seller prize.** See E.1.
- Each extra draw date may count toward the decree's two-dates-per-month cap [P] (#13).

### B.5 Reglamento, tickets, printing
**Typical reglamento content** [U]:
- organizer ID and contact
- prize and delivery
- price, quantity and sales cut-off
- draw source (lottery, ordinary draw number, date, digits)
- suspension rule
- unsold-number rule
- reservation, payment and refund rules
- winner claim deadline
- public delivery
- dispute handling
- data notice

An official Lotería de Bogotá page says authorized raffle tickets show number, public price, place/date/time of draw, lottery name and prize description [S, snippet] (#15).

**Ticket design** [U]:
- Print the number on both ticket and colilla.
- Add a batch or serial ID and perforation.
- Add organizer, price and draw info.
- Add a QR to the public grid.
- Add a seller-code box and a unique stamp or ink per batch.
- The seller signs the colilla.
- A talonario helps seller control; keep the stubs [S] (#23).
- Digital tickets: image or PDF with a unique code and QR, with verification against the master sheet.

**Printing prices:**
- Creativos Publicidad (Bogotá): COP 85,000–210,000 for 100–1,000 units, 15×7 or 21.5×7 cm, perforation, numbering and glued talonario, 2 business days, 50% deposit [P] (#24).
- Tarjetas Innovadoras: 1,000 boletas 18×12 cm at COP 187,991, listed down from 336,589 [S, snippet] (#25).
- No Fusagasugá printer price found [U].

### B.6 Boards, streaming, delivery
- **Number boards.**
  - Google Sheets "Publicar en la Web" republishes automatically, with updates "pueden tardar unos minutos" [P] (#26).
  - Apps with public boards: MisRifas, Mi Rifa App with 2–4 digit boards [S] (#27).
  - Recommendation: one master sheet, a fixed daily image time, and a timestamped final snapshot.
  - Daily image options [U]: sheet screenshot, a small script, or Canva.
- **Streaming and delivery.** Stream the lottery's own broadcast, such as Boyacá on Facebook Live [S] (#10), and photograph the official results page. Live streaming and photos are standard advice [S] (#22). Decreto 1486 requires notarized proof of prize delivery for future authorizations [P] (#13). Recommendation: copy that with a signed *acta de entrega* and a video.
- **Common mistakes** [S] (#19):
  - inconsistent dates across channels
  - false urgency
  - changing method at the last minute
  - delayed results or delivery

## D. Payments

### D.1 Bre-B
- It is the Banco de la República's interoperable instant-payment system, available "segundo semestre de 2025" [P] (#41).
- Bancolombia dates the official start to 6 Oct (2025) [S] (#42).
- Keys opened 14 Jul 2025, with 128 entities [S].
- Llaves for individuals are cédula, cell number, email and alphanumeric (@, 5–20 characters). One key maps to one account, and an account can hold several keys [P] (#41, #43).
- Registration and transfers are free. The limit is 1,000 UVB = COP 12,110,000 per transaction in 2026, and entities may set lower limits [P] (#41).
- Nequi caps Bre-B sends at COP 3,000,000 per transaction [P] (#43).
- Bancolombia cites a COP 3M daily cap and a 20-second settlement time [S] (#42).
- By June 2026 there were 108M keys and 5M daily transactions. QR for individuals was due in H1 2026 [P] (#41).
- To receive money: register a llave in your app (in Nequi, Bre-B → Registrar) and share it. Funds arrive in the account [P] (#43).

### D.2 Caps and fees (2026)

| | Facts |
|---|---|
| Nequi bajo monto | 210.5 UVT = COP 11,024,727.50 per month for movements and for balance. 4x1000 exempt on withdrawals up to 65 UVT = COP 3,404,310. The cuenta de ahorros Nequi has no monthly cap, and its exemption is up to 350 UVT = COP 18,330,900 [P] (#43). |
| Nequi fees | Nequi→Nequi/Bancolombia, ACH, QR and Bre-B sends are free. Withdrawals at Bancolombia ATMs and corresponsales are free [P] (#43). |

Derived:

- Recommendation: use a dedicated account, or a cuenta de ahorros.

### D.3 QR and fake receipts
- **QR.**
  - Nequi QR Negocios and llave de negocio: no fixed monthly fee, no per-movement cost, no daily or monthly receiving limit, minimum COP 1. They accept other banks via Bre-B [P] (#43).
  - They run through the Nequi Negocios app, and individual requirements are not stated [U].
  - Recommendation: print a fixed-amount QR (COP 6,000) on the grid and tickets.
- **Fake comprobantes.** Nequi's own guidance [P] (#43):
  - Scan the receipt's QR from the "$ → Comprobar pago" option in the app, not the camera. No login is needed and the QR is valid 5 minutes.
  - Genuine receipts cannot be edited.
  - Failed payments show "ANULADO" and no QR.
  - Users cannot reverse payments.
  - Movements of the last 30 days are always available, so doubt anyone who cannot regenerate the receipt.
  - Daviplata: "código QR → confirmar comprobante" [S] (#46).
  - SMS can be forged, and fake wallet apps are used [S] (#46).
- Recommendation:
  - Release a number only after the credit appears in your own app.
  - Match payer name, amount, time and reference.
  - Never accept screenshots.
  - Log the reference in the sheet.
- **Separation.**
  - Nequi allows up to 10 Bolsillos, plus Metas and Colchón [P] (#43).
  - They organize money inside the same account, so the account cap still applies [U].
  - Recommendation: for cash, count with two people and deposit within 24 h.

## E. Sellers and bookkeeping

### E.1 Commission norms
I found no authoritative Colombian norm [U]. Guides mention "a small commission per talonario sold" with no figure [S] (#23). Raffle apps advertise seller and affiliate features [S] (#27). Recommendation: 10% flat, or one free number per 10 sold (also 10% of sales value) [derived]. At COP 6,000 that is COP 600 per ticket, or COP 60,000 per 100-ticket talonario.

**Net proceeds** (prize COP 2.0M, printing COP 200k, commission on all sales) [derived]:

| Tickets sold | 0% | 10% | 15% |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1,000 | 3.80M | 3.20M | 2.90M |
| 900 | 3.20M | 2.66M | 2.39M |
| 800 | 2.60M | 2.12M | 1.88M |
| 700 | 2.00M | 1.58M | 1.37M |

- Net of COP 3.0M needs at least 867 tickets at 0% commission, or 963 at 10%. At 15% it is infeasible (1,020).
- If the 14% derechos (COP 840,000) applied, the best case falls to COP 2.96M, or 2.36M at 10% commission.

**Seller prize for the winning number:** no norm found [U]. Recommendation: a small fixed bono (COP 50,000–100,000), pre-announced, with a stub cut-off 24–48 h before the draw to stop back-dated "sales".

### E.2 Settlement and controls [U]
- Issue talonarios of 100 numbers, signed for, one at a time.
- Hold a deposit or ID copy for each talonario.
- Do a weekly *liquidación*:
  - stubs returned in order
  - amount owed = sold × 6,000 − commission
  - paid by transfer with a reference or by cash counted by two people
- A number is marked "Pagado" only after the money is verified.
- Reconcile all stubs against the master sheet before the draw.

### E.3 Practical setup (Google Sheets)
1. **NUMEROS** (000–999): número, estado, comprador, teléfono, vendedor, canal, medio de pago, referencia, fecha-hora de apartado, fecha-hora de pago, talonario/colilla.
2. **PAGOS**: id, fecha-hora, número(s), valor, pagador, medio, referencia, verificado por, conciliado.
3. **VENDEDORES**: talonario y rango, entregadas, vendidas, devueltas, por cobrar, pagado, comisión, saldo.
4. **GASTOS**: fecha, concepto, proveedor, valor, foto del soporte, cuenta.
5. **RESUMEN**: bruto (pagadas × 6,000) against deposits, comisiones, gastos, premio, neto.

- **Daily reconciliation (about 10 minutes):**
  - match each credit to a sale
  - flag unmatched credits "por identificar" and do not assign a number yet
  - check that paid count × price equals matched credits plus cash deposits
  - check duplicates
  - lock edits at cut-off
  - save a timestamped PDF and image before the draw
- **Final transparency report:** tickets sold, gross, commissions, itemized expenses with receipts, prize with proof of delivery, net and its use. Use initials for buyers. Add a data-privacy notice [U].

## Key implications for this raffle
1. Clear the legal screen before spending: cash prizes and used goods are barred by Decreto 1486, an operador is required, and a 14% levy (COP 840,000) would apply [P/U].
2. Draw with Bogotá (Thu 23:15) or Boyacá (Sat 22:30), which have no Q4 moves. Name the "sorteo ordinario N.º" and exclude extraordinarios.
3. Reglamento: "premio mayor, 4 cifras, últimas 3 cifras; ignora serie, secos y lanzamiento adicional." Include a postponement rule: next ordinary draw of the same lottery.
4. Pick the unsold-winning-number rule before the first sale. Recommendation: roll to the next ordinary draw once, or refund.
5. The 300-number mini-raffle is mathematically unfair as drafted. Use 250 or 200 numbers, or drop it.
6. Net COP 3.0M needs at least 867 tickets sold at 0% commission, or 963 at 10%. Cap commissions at 10% and push direct sales.
7. Use one dedicated account, preferably a Bre-B llave plus QR. Nequi bajo monto caps at COP 11.02M per month, with 4x1000 exempt up to COP 3.4M.
8. Verify payments only inside your own app, never from screenshots. Use Nequi's QR check (5-minute window) for receipts.
9. WhatsApp Business app is enough: opt-in, small batches, no mass blasting. Meta and TikTok paid promotion is effectively closed, and Facebook group rules need a check.
10. Run everything from one Google Sheet with a published grid and a daily image. Airtable Free caps at 1,000 records, Glide public apps need Plus ($50/month), and raffle SaaS does not make a raffle legal.
11. Printing is cheap (about COP 85,000–210,000 for 1,000 tickets with stub). Number both parts and keep the stubs.
12. Sellers: 10% flat or one free number per 10 sold, 100-number talonarios, weekly liquidación, no fiado, and a 24–48 h cut-off.
13. Livestream the lottery, screenshot the official results page, publish the final snapshot and a transparency report, and deliver the prize on camera with an *acta*.
14. Prefer a ≤125 cc bike. It is tax-exempt in Cundinamarca, A1 suffices, and SOAT is COP 256,200–343,300.
15. Confirm the license route locally before budgeting. The CALE exam regime may add about COP 0.56–0.62M on top of the course.

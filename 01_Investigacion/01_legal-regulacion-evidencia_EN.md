# Research file: legal, regulatory, tax, enforcement and evidence review for a proposed 1,000-ticket raffle in Fusagasugá (as of 2 Oct 2026)

**Reliability note.** I read the statute and decree texts directly from PDFs: Ley 643/2001, Decreto 1968/2001, Código Penal, Estatuto Tributario, Ley 1393/2010 and Ley 1801/2016. Decreto 1486/2024 was a scanned PDF with OCR gaps, so I cross-checked it against DIAN's compilation. Pages that I could only get through a summarizer or search-engine summary are tagged [S] or [U]. Colombian statutes are quoted because the brief asked for it. News and web sources are paraphrased. This is research, not legal advice.

**Tags:** [P] official or primary, [S] reputable secondary, [U] unverified or inferred. Sources are cited as (#n).

## Headline findings

- The draft (cash prize, no authorization) is unlawful on four separate grounds. Cash prizes are banned. Only authorized operators may sell raffles. Unauthorized operation is a crime under C.P. art. 312 (6–8 years). A 33% payout is also below the 40% minimum prize plan. [P] (#1, #4, #7)
- Raffle rules were rewritten by Decreto 1486 of 13 Dec 2024. It creates operators and gestores, requires electronic sales, sets the prize plan at ≥40% of emission, and caps raffles at two per month. Paper-ticket raffles were allowed only during a 12-month transition. [P] (#4, #5) A Coljuegos-focused outlet says that window ran to 10 Aug 2026 [S] (#21). I found no official extension and no authorized operator [U].
- On a COP 6,000,000 emission, a compliant raffle needs a new in-kind prize of at least COP 2,400,000. Derechos de explotación are COP 840,000, paid upfront. The insurance guarantee must equal the prize. Best-case net is roughly COP 2.4–2.55M. [P/U calc]
- Documented enforcement targets influencers and organized networks. No de minimis rule exists in the text. The Corte Suprema affirmed convictions for a used-car/cash raffle and a school-bond raffle in 2011. [P/S] (#14, #15)
- Taxes are minor. A prize of 48 UVT or less (COP 2,513,952 in 2026) carries no withholding. [P/S] (#11, #51, #53)
- I could not find Fusagasugá's own raffle procedure online. The compiled Estatuto de Rentas lists "Derechos de explotación de rifas" as repealed in 2020. [P] (#38)

## A. Legal framework

### A1. Constitución, art. 336
- "Ningún monopolio podrá establecerse sino como arbitrio rentístico, con una finalidad de interés público o social y en virtud de la ley." [P] (#4 recital; #6)
- "Las rentas obtenidas en el ejercicio de los monopolios de suerte y azar estarán destinadas exclusivamente a los servicios de salud." [P] (#4; #6)
- "La evasión fiscal en materia de rentas provenientes de monopolios rentísticos será sancionada penalmente en los términos que establezca la ley." This is the constitutional hook for C.P. art. 312. [S] (#6)

### A3. Regulation that governs raffles now
Decreto 1968/2001 was compiled into Decreto 1068/2015, Libro 2, Parte 7, Título 3 (arts. 2.7.3.1–2.7.3.12). It was then modified by **Decreto 1486 of 13 Dec 2024**, in force from publication. [P] (#4, #5) My reading that 1968 is now superseded in practice is an inference [U]. Art. 2.7.3.3 on competent authorities was left unchanged. [P] (#4)

|---|---|---|

The **gestor** (natural or legal person) registers each raffle through the operator's online real-time platform. Gestores may sell "de manera física o electrónica a través de una plataforma web o de redes sociales". |
| Manual sales | Not addressed | "Está prohibida la operación y venta manual de rifas, salvo que las boletas sean emitidas por una terminal de venta electrónica". **Transitional paragraph:** during the 12-month transition the competent entity may approve physical-ticket, manual-sale raffles. |
| Application lead time | ≥45 calendar days (art. 5) | ≥30 calendar days |
| Application content | Name and address, cédula and certificado judicial for natural persons, raffle name, lottery, date/time/place, ticket price, total tickets, tickets participating, total emission, detailed prize plan with commercial value including IVA | Same, plus registration through the operator |
| Prize ownership | "Comprobante de la plena propiedad sin reserva de dominio"; invoices or avalúo (art. 6) | Same |
| Guarantee (póliza) | Issued by an insurer, equal to the total prize plan, valid ≥4 months after the draw | Same; may be replaced by a bank guarantee |
| Draw | "Están prohibidas las rifas que no utilicen los resultados de la lotería tradicional" | Two options: digits of a legal traditional lottery's premio mayor, or a certified random-number generator (GNA, ≤6 digits, up to 1,000,000 numbers). Manual transitional raffles must use the lottery. |

| Derechos | 14% of gross, paid at authorization on 100% of tickets issued, adjusted after the draw | Same, paid through the operator |

**Mandatory ticket content** (Decreto 1968 art. 6.4, kept in 1486) [P] (#3, #4):
- ticket number and sale price;
- place, date and time of the draw, and the lottery used;
- prize expiry term;
- a space for the authorizing act's number and date;
- description of the prizes with brand and model, and their value;
- name, address, ID and signature of the person responsible;
- raffle name;
- whether the prize is payable to the bearer.

Decreto 1486 adds bar codes or QR codes. Applications also need the advertising text, which must follow the authority's image manual, and the authorization of the lottery whose results are used.

**Coljuegos instruments on the new model** (mostly [S]):
- Technical-requirements annex, Resolución 20250017074, published on Coljuegos' site 3 Sep 2025 [P] (#20); reported as dated 21 Aug 2025 [S] (#24).
- Resolución 20250018494 of 22 Aug 2025, the legal and financial requirements for technological operators [S] (#22).
- A March 2026 draft would cut required working capital from COP 5,100M to COP 2,380M (consultation 2–9 Mar 2026). I could not confirm it was finalized. [S] (#22, #23)
- The 12-month transition runs from entry into force of the technical requirements. Coverage of the March 2026 draft says it "contaría hasta el 10 de agosto de 2026". [P] for the decree (#4); [S] for the date (#21). I found no extension [U].
- Coljuegos' monthly list of authorized raffles shows only two companies for Jan–Sep 2026: Grupo Empresarial en Línea (Paga Todo) and Grupo de Entretenimiento La Única. It lists about 20 authorizations and no individuals. [P] (#26)

### A4. Cash prizes
Cash prizes are not allowed. Art. 27 requires "premios en especie", and Decreto 1068 art. 2.7.3.2 bans "las rifas con premios en dinero". [P] (#1, #4)

A raffle that pays cash against the last digits of an official lottery is not a legal "rifa". It is an unauthorized game of chance under art. 5 ("premio, en dinero o en especie") and art. 4(g). Structurally it resembles "chance" (art. 21), which only concessionaires chosen by public bidding may run. The classification as chance is my inference [U].

Authorized raffles use restricted gift cards as an in-kind prize. One national raffle's terms include a COP 58,000,000 debit gift card usable only in shops, not at ATMs or for cash withdrawals. [P] (#60) Whether a municipality would accept this for a small raffle is unverified.

### A5. Código Penal art. 312 and case law
Current text, as modified by Ley 1393/2010 art. 18 (earlier by Ley 1142/2007 and Decreto 130/2010) [P] (#7, #8):
> "El que de cualquier manera o valiéndose de cualquier medio ejerza una actividad establecida como monopolio de arbitrio rentístico, sin la respectiva autorización, permiso o contrato, o utilice elementos o modalidades de juego no oficiales, incurrirá en prisión de seis (6) a ocho (8) años y multa de quinientos (500) a mil (1.000) salarios mínimos legales mensuales vigentes."

The penalty rises by one third for concessionaires and up to one half for public servants. At the 2026 SMMLV of COP 1,750,905 (#50), the fine ranges from COP 875,452,500 to COP 1,750,905,000. [S/U calc] I found no later amendment. A secondary compilation shows the same text updated 17 Sep 2026 [S].

**Case law found:**

|---|---|---|

The Court affirmed the conviction of 36 months plus a fine of 100 SMLMV (under the older penalty). It rejected the argument of no fiscal harm. The protected interest is the "orden económico y social" and bettors' confidence. No insignificance doctrine was discussed. | [S] (#14) |

| Corte Constitucional C-1114/01 | Upheld the ban on permanent raffles and the 14% rate. | [S] (#13) |

I found no Corte Suprema or Corte Constitucional decision after 2011 on small-scale or charity raffles. I found no de minimis or lesividad exception. [U] These are old cases with lower penalties then and larger or organized operations.

### A6. Administrative and police exposure
- **Coljuegos and administrators of the monopoly.** Under Ley 643 art. 44 (#2), the administering entity suspends the game, issues a "liquidación de aforo" plus a 200% sanction on derechos, may close premises, refers the case to criminal authorities, and bars the person for five years. Ley 1393 art. 21 allows coactive collection. [P] (#8) Coljuegos publicly states a fine of up to 100 SMMLV plus disqualification. That is COP 175,090,500 at 2026 values. [P] (#27) The detailed fine schedule in Ley 1393 art. 20 comes only from a summary [S] (#9).
- **Illustration.** On a COP 6M emission, derechos are COP 840,000. Aforo plus a 200% sanction would be about COP 2.52M, before any fine. [U calc]
- **Competence.** Coljuegos authorizes only national-scope raffles (#18). For single-municipality raffles the administrator is the municipality (art. 28). Ley 1393 art. 19 leaves administration of derechos and gastos with the territorial administrator. [P] (#8)
- **Código Nacional de Seguridad y Convivencia (Ley 1801/2016).** I did not find a specific behavior or fine for unauthorized raffles in the text I searched. [U] What the code does provide:
  - Art. 205 makes the alcalde "primera autoridad de Policía", empowered to "Autorizar, directamente o a través de su delegado, la realización de juegos, rifas y espectáculos" and to suspend them when they involve complex crowds. These were numerals 9–10 in the original text. [P] (#10)
  - Art. 162 lets alcaldes order written searches of "sitios abiertos al público" to obtain evidence of "casas de juego o establecimiento que funcione contra la ley". [P] (#10)
  - Art. 206 covers Inspectores de Policía, who are the municipal police authorities.
- **At municipal level.** The acting authorities are the alcalde and Secretaría de Gobierno or Hacienda, Inspección de Policía, the Policía Nacional, and the Fiscalía for crimes. [P]/[U]

### A7. Fraud if the prize is unpaid (C.P. art. 246, as modified by Ley 1474/2011)
- Base penalty: prisión de 2 a 8 años and multa of 50–1,000 SMMLV for "provecho ilícito … induciendo o manteniendo a otro en error por medio de artificios o engaños". [P] (#7)
- It also covers a person who in "lotería, rifa o juego" obtains benefit by fraudulent means "para asegurar un determinado resultado". [P] (#7)
- Where the amount does not exceed 10 SMMLV: 1–2 years and a fine up to 10 SMMLV. [P] (#7)
- Art. 267 raises penalties by one third to one half when the value exceeds 100 SMMLV. [P] (#7)

### A9. Personal data (Ley 1581 de 2012), brief
- Scope: art. 2 covers personal data in any database, excluding files kept in an "ámbito exclusivamente personal o doméstico". [S] (#12)
- Minimum obligations: art. 9 (prior informed authorization), art. 12 (tell data subjects the purpose, their rights and your identity), art. 17 (internal policy, handling of queries, security against loss or unauthorized access). [S] (#12)
- Art. 25 (database registry) and art. 23 (fines up to 2,000 SMMLV, imposed by the SIC). [S] (#12)
- A commercial list of about 1,000 buyers with phone numbers probably falls outside the household exemption. [U]
- Whether Decreto 090/2018 (the registry-size threshold) exempts an individual was not verified. [U]

## C. The legal path in Fusagasugá

**Which office.** By law the municipality authorizes raffles operated only inside it. [P] (#1 art. 28; #4 art. 2.7.3.3) In other municipalities the trámite usually sits with the Secretaría de Gobierno (the Leiva statute names it; #39). Other Cundinamarca municipalities such as Tocancipá publish a SUIT trámite. A search summary describes a written request ≥45 days ahead, 15 business days for review, and a 14% fee. [S] (#40)

For Fusagasugá specifically:
- I could not read the Alcaldía's trámite pages (JavaScript-rendered, #38 portal pointer: fusagasuga.gov.co/tema/tramites-y-servicios).
- A search summary claimed Fusagasugá lists SUIT trámites on raffles. I could not confirm this. [U]
- The compiled Estatuto de Rentas (Decreto 129 of 2024, compiling Acuerdo 026/2016) lists "Derechos de explotación de rifas" as "Derogado mediante Art. 3 del Acuerdo 19 del 24 de diciembre del 2020". [P] (#38) I read this as meaning no local fee schedule beyond national law, but that is inference [U].
- The Alcaldía's published contact: Calle 6 No. 6-24, atencionalciudadano@fusagasuga-cundinamarca.gov.co. [P] (#38)
- If tickets are sold beyond Fusagasugá (WhatsApp and social media make this likely), competence shifts to the department's SCPD or to Coljuegos. I could not confirm which entity acts as Cundinamarca's SCPD for raffles. [U inference]

**Mechanics that add risk:**
- Only tickets sold participate, because unsold ones are invalidated the business day before. If the winning number is unsold, the prize must be re-raffled through the postponement procedure. [P] (#3, #4)
- The second mini-raffle (300 × COP 2,000, prize COP 200,000) fails the prize-plan test (33%). Its derechos would be COP 84,000.

**Feasibility.** The legal route is open in theory but financially thin and procedurally uncertain: new-only prize, upfront capital of about COP 3.4M, and the post-transition operator/electronic-sales model. With one of the largest local raffles as the goal, a compliant version nets about COP 2.4M at best, which may not cover the motorcycle (price not researched here). [U]

## Key implications for this raffle

1. Do not run the draft as designed. It conflicts with the cash-prize ban, the authorization requirement, and (even if authorizable) the 40% prize-plan floor. [P]
2. Criminal exposure is real on paper. Art. 312 carries 6–8 years and a fine of COP 875M–1.75B at 2026 values, with no de minimis exception in the text. [P] The Corte Suprema affirmed convictions in a used-car/cash raffle and a school-bond raffle (2009–2011, at lower penalties). [S]
3. Documented enforcement targets influencers and organized networks. For a one-off 1,000-ticket raffle the likelihood of criminal action cannot be estimated. [U] Visibility is the main driver. A goal of being among the largest local raffles, public social-media posting, shops as sellers and unpaid-prize complaints all raise exposure.
4. Administrative exposure is separate. It covers aforo plus a 200% sanction on derechos (about COP 2.5M on a COP 6M emission [U calc]), a fine Coljuegos puts at up to 100 SMMLV (COP 175M), and a five-year bar. [P/S]
5. A legal route exists only for sales confined to Fusagasugá. Selling by WhatsApp or social media to buyers elsewhere likely moves competence to the department or Coljuegos. [U inference from art. 28]
6. The new regime requires operators and electronic sales. Paper-ticket raffles were allowed only during a transition reported to end 10 Aug 2026. I found no extension and no authorized operator, so ask the Alcaldía and Coljuegos in writing whether a paper-ticket raffle can still be authorized. [P]/[S]
7. A compliant version needs a new in-kind prize bought upfront (at least COP 2.4M), COP 840,000 of derechos at authorization, and an insurance guarantee equal to the prize. That is about COP 3.4M upfront for a net of about COP 2.4–2.55M if everything sells. Compare that with the motorcycle's price. [U calc]
8. If you proceed on the legal path, keep the prize at or below 48 UVT (COP 2,513,952) to avoid organizer withholding duties. A restricted gift card is how authorized raffles offer money-like prizes. [P/S]
9. Unsold numbers create no-winner draws, so the prize must be re-raffled. Only sold tickets participate, and the organizer cannot hold tickets on draw day. [P]
10. Never sell to minors (Ley 643 art. 4(b)). Family and friends as sellers, and especially shops, add exposure, because authorities may close establishments. [P]
11. If you keep buyer names and phones, comply with Ley 1581: consent, notice and security. The payment trail also becomes durable evidence of operation. [S/U]
12. Fraud exposure (art. 246) applies to unpaid or rigged raffles, with 2–8 years, or 1–2 years if the amount is ≤10 SMMLV. [P]
13. The pro-fondos label gives no legal shelter. The art. 5 exclusions are narrow and exclude lucrative exploitation. [P]
14. Lower-risk alternatives include Vaki crowdfunding (about 10% fees, and likely not fee-free for a personal vehicle purchase). [P/U] Before any step, get written answers from the Alcaldía de Fusagasugá and Coljuegos (contactenos@coljuegos.gov.co, 01 8000 18 28 88 [S] (#25)), and consult a lawyer.

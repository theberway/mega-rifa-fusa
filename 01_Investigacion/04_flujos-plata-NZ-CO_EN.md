# Raffle cross-border cash flows: research brief (Fri 2 Oct 2026)

Tags: [P] official, [S] secondary, [U] unverified or derived. (#n) = Sources.

## Bottom line
- COP 2,000,000 ≈ NZD 1,085 (≈ USD 605). NZ ticket proceeds (NZD 150–300) cover only 14–28% of an NZ-winner payout; shortfall ≈ NZD 785–935 (≈ COP 1.45–1.72M).
- NZ→Colombia: only Wise (~1.6%, extrapolated) and Remitly (flat NZD 6.49; free on first transfer) could be priced. Western Union and WorldRemit unverified.
- No provider page names Nu Colombia; a Wise guide says Nu is domestic-only [S]. Plan on Nequi or Bancolombia, then Bre-B to Nu.
- Colombia→NZ is the weak leg. Don't pre-fund it; send only if an NZ resident wins.

## 3. Colombia → NZ
- Nu: domestic-only per Wise guide [S] (#11). Use Bancolombia, another bank, or a licensed remitter.
- Global66: 4% fee; destinations listed exclude NZ [S] (#10).
- Wise from COP: unconfirmed. Paysend CO→NZ page exists (#12); Bancolombia international transfers (#13); fees not retrieved. Budget 2–4% + flat fees [U].
- FX rules: through intermediarios del mercado cambiario (banks, casas de cambio), declaración de cambio per Banrep DCIP-83 (#14, #15) [S].

## 4. NZ student payments
Bank transfers by account number: ASB credits within an hour (9am–midnight) (#16); ANZ same day before 10pm (#17); most settle within ~2 h (#18). No live national instant-payment scheme found (#18, #19). Use ticket number as payment reference + ledger.

## 5. Colombian receiving side
- Nequi: low-amount accounts receive up to USD 2,000/month from abroad; savings Nequi up to USD 5,000/month (#21). Low-amount cap COP 11,024,727/month (#23).
- Bre-B: domestic only; ≈ COP 12.11M per operation (#22); COP 3M per movement in Nequi (#24).
- Tax: no tax found on receiving a transfer; GMF 4x1000 on debits (exempt account up to COP 18.33M/month) (#23).

## 6. Recommended flows
**(a) Colombian winner**: before sales, get live Wise/Remitly quotes (no money moves). After the draw Santiago pays COP 2M domestically. Sofía sends one lump NZD 150–300, bank-funded: Remitly if first transfer/promo, else lower live quote (likely Wise); payout to Nequi or Bancolombia, then Bre-B to Nu. Cost ≈ 0–2% + 0.4% GMF.
**(b) NZ winner**: rule = NZD amount fixed in advance; Sofía pays by NZ bank transfer using proceeds + her advance; Santiago reimburses after the winner is known. (Project decision: reimburse in COP to Sofía's Colombian account via Bre-B → no cross-border leg.) Keep draw record, rate screenshot, receipts.
